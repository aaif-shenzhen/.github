# AAIF SZ Chapter 

## About AAIF Shenzhen Chapter 关于 AAIF 深圳分会

The AAIF Shenzhen chapter serves as a vibrant hub for the local agentic AI community. We bring together developers, researchers, and builders to discuss practical implementations, share learnings from the product environments, and foster collaboration on key open-source agentic AI projects. Our events prioritize hands-on demos and technical deep dives, ensuring that the Shenzhen community remains at the forefront of agentic AI innovation.

【AAIF深圳分会】是当地人工智能领域的活跃交流中心。我们汇聚了开发者、研究人员和构建者，共同探讨实际应用问题，分享在相关产品环境中的经验，并促进关键开源人工智能项目的合作。我们的活动注重实践演示和技术深度探讨，确保深圳的社区能够始终处于人工智能创新的最前沿。

## Local Chapter Vibes 本地分会氛围

Our events are built for builders. We prioritize signal over noise, focusing on technical deep dives, practical demos, and honest conversations about what works in production. It's an intimate, curated, and vendor-neutral space where the community gathers to share real artifacts, solve hard problems together, and build the future of agentic AI in real time.

我们的活动专为开发者打造。我们注重实质内容而非冗余信息，聚焦于技术深度探讨、实用演示，以及关于生产环境中有效方案的坦诚交流。这是一个氛围亲密、精心策划且供应商中立的空间，社区成员在此汇聚，分享真实的成果，共同解决棘手的问题，并实时构建自主人工智能的未来。
